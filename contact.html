Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions our clients ask before beginning their project with InvenTech.

How quickly can InvenTech kick off our project?

Once we finalize your requirements and scope, we typically kick off development within 3 to 5 business days. We begin with architectural planning and UI/UX wireframes so you see tangible progress from sprint one.

Do you offer a free consultation and project estimate?

Yes, absolutely. We provide a complimentary 30-minute technical consultation where our senior engineer evaluates your requirements, suggests the optimal tech stack, and provides a transparent cost and timeline estimate.

Can we sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)?

Yes, 100%. We take client privacy and proprietary intellectual property very seriously. We are glad to sign a mutual NDA before you share any confidential business details or workflows.

How does communication work during development?

You get direct communication with your dedicated project lead via WhatsApp, Slack, email, and scheduled video demo calls. We work in agile 1-2 week sprints, sharing live staging demos at each step.

What post-launch support and maintenance do you provide?

Every project includes a complimentary warranty period post-deployment for bug fixes and adjustments. We also offer affordable monthly maintenance plans covering security updates, server monitoring, and feature enhancements.
