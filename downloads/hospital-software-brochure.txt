InvenTech — Hospital & Medical Management Software (Brochure)

Overview
- A modern hospital management system for clinics, hospitals, and medical centers.
- Designed to streamline OPD, IPD, lab, pharmacy, emergency, and specialized departments.

Core Modules
- Reception & Registration (MRN, tokens/queue)
- OPD (appointments, consultations, prescriptions)
- IPD & Ward (admission, bed management, discharge)
- Emergency (triage, rapid processing)
- Laboratory (orders, samples, results, reports)
- Pharmacy (inventory, purchase, sales, expiry)
- Billing & Accounts (invoices, payments, reports)
- Dental (tooth charting, treatment plans)
- Eye/Optometry (refraction, prescriptions)
- Staff & Roles (RBAC, permissions, audit trails)
